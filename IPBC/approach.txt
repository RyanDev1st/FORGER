A summarization of my idea to the IPBC framework

I- Inspiration
P- Practice
B- Brainstorm
C- Create

Inspiration:
    Search and research from internet resources. Then read and synthesize the ideas from all sources you find

Practice: Internally reason through with the synthesis. Then record your findings, the usual "AHA!" kind of finds. 

After 50 rounds of practice, continue with Brainstorm 

Brainstorm: You have gotten a firm gasp of the subject. You are now on chaotic exploratory mode. Your job is to brainstorm and seek new methodologies on your own

Have atleast one round, X turns of Lingcoln Style Debate with another subagent of the same tonnage (e.g. Opus 4.7 effort high versus Opus 4.7 effort high)

Create:
-Synthesize and conclude your finding in Brainstorm 
-Come up with an implementation plan. 
-Implement and adjust the implementation plan accordingly to the findings during runtime.
