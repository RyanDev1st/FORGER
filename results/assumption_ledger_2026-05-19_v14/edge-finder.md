# edge-finder-assumption-ledger

## Mission
Find assumptions normal lanes miss: frame assumptions, regional assumptions, historical assumptions, and adjacent-domain assumptions that break transfer.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field analogues.
2. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
3. Non-English or regional practice.
4. Niche expert forums.
5. Substantive contrarian mechanisms.

## Search Procedure
1. Identify likely shared assumptions.
2. Search domains where those assumptions fail.
3. Search historical reversals and abandoned paradigms.
4. Search regional or non-English counterconditions.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 cards.

## Assumption Card Gate
Each card must include:
- assumption type
- assumption text
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- invalidation trigger
- impact if false
- confidence
