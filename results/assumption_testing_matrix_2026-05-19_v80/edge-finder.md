# edge-assumption-testing-matrix

Edge lane for Assumption Testing Matrix.

## Input

Brief and `assumption-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Find weird, neglected, socially uncomfortable, or cross-domain assumptions that normal lanes may leave untested.

## Selection rule

Prefer sources with:

- dissent later proven useful
- adjacent-domain failures with matching mechanism
- hidden stakeholder harms, incentive traps, or reputational risks
- dead prototypes or abandoned tools that reveal invisible constraints
- named or track-record pseudonymous practitioners

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding E<n>: <edge assumption finding>
- Assumption id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Edge assumption:
- Why normal lanes may miss it:
- Importance: low | medium | high | critical
- Existing evidence: none | weak | moderate | strong | unknown
- Failure mechanism if false:
- Kill criterion:
- Mitigation or reframe:
- Gate decision: pass | test | downgrade | reject | re-search
```

## Drop rules

Drop paranoia, persecution narratives, unfalsifiable warnings, novelty-only objections, and assumptions with no plausible mechanism or kill criterion.

## Closing

List uncomfortable assumptions, hidden stakeholders, weird failure paths, kill criteria, and residual risks that should remain visible.
