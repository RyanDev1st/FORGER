# scholar-assumption-testing-matrix

Academic lane for Assumption Testing Matrix.

## Input

Brief and `assumption-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Find which methodological, causal, measurement, or population assumptions are critical and under-evidenced before synthesis treats them as reliable.

## Selection rule

Prefer sources with:

- assumption tests, hypothesis tests, limitations, negative results, or replication signals
- measurement-validity or construct-validity discussion
- causal warnings and population-transfer limits
- systematic reviews, methods papers, or stable datasets
- quotable text and stable identifiers

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <scholar assumption finding>
- Assumption id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Academic assumption:
- Importance: low | medium | high | critical
- Existing evidence: none | weak | moderate | strong | unknown
- Evidence supports assumption:
- Evidence weakens assumption:
- Test or evidence needed:
- Gate decision: pass | test | downgrade | reject | re-search
- Confidence: high | medium | low
```

## Drop rules

Drop generic caveats, unfalsifiable skepticism, and sources that do not affect method, measurement, population, or causal strength.

## Closing

List critical academic assumptions, tested assumptions, downgraded claims, and evidence gaps that must remain visible in synthesis.
