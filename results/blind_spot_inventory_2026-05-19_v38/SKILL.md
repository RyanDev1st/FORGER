# gnosis-blind-spot-inventory

## Invocation
Use when what is missing may matter as much as what is found: under-studied groups, inaccessible archives, unpublished failures, language gaps, measurement blind spots, and selection-biased search results.

## Pipeline

### Step 1 — Parse Absence Frame
Extract:
- central question
- expected evidence types
- missing stakeholder groups
- likely unpublished evidence
- inaccessible or non-indexed sources
- measurement gaps
- decision risk from absence

### Step 2 — Spawn Lanes
Spawn Scholar, Community, and Edge in parallel. Each lane emits blind-spot cards plus findings.

### Step 3 — Validate
Run V1–V8:
- V1 file exists and non-empty.
- V2 at least 5 blind-spot cards or pivot log.
- V3 no top-level refusal.
- V4 every card has source and verbatim quote or explicit absence log.
- V5 every card names absent evidence zone.
- V6 every card names likely bias mechanism.
- V7 every card recommends accept gap, proxy, search deeper, or block conclusion.
- V8 closing block present.

### Step 4 — Audit
Check URLs, quote matches, absence-log specificity, and Edge redundancy. Preserve failures as downgrade flags.

### Step 5 — Build Blind Spot Map
Classify cards into:
- absent population
- unpublished negative
- inaccessible archive
- language gap
- measurement gap
- survivorship bias
- platform bias
- proxy evidence zone

### Step 6 — Synthesize
Return:
- what evidence exists
- what evidence is missing
- which absences matter
- which proxy evidence can stand in
- conclusion limits caused by blind spots

### Step 7 — Re-fan
Re-fan only when a blind spot could overturn the conclusion or block safe recommendation. One round max unless user escalates.

## Output
Return:
1. Blind spot inventory.
2. Accept gap/proxy/search deeper/block decisions.
3. Current synthesis.
4. Missing-evidence hot spots.
5. Proxy options.
6. Evidence appendix and absence log.

## Constraints
- No strong conclusion over decision-critical absence.
- No generic unknowns; every blind spot must name likely source zone or bias mechanism.
- Maximum four concurrent threads.
- Preserve raw evidence appendices.
