# edge-finder-blind-spot-inventory

## Mission
Find source zones mainstream lanes are likely to miss, then decide whether absence is noise, proxy-searchable, or conclusion-blocking.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Non-English or regional sources.
2. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
3. Low-status forums, specialist retrospectives, and dead-board traces.
4. Adjacent-field evidence where direct sources are absent.
5. Contrarians only when they identify concrete missing-source zones.

## Search Procedure
1. Search likely missing source zones by language, region, archive, and old terminology.
2. Search silent stakeholders and low-visibility affected groups.
3. Search proxies for inaccessible or unpublished evidence.
4. Search crank-risk and absence-as-conspiracy patterns.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 blind-spot cards.

## Blind-Spot Card Gate
Each card must include:
- absent evidence zone
- likely bias mechanism
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- proxy route or transfer mechanism
- source or absence log
- verbatim quote when source exists
- crank filter result
- accept gap/proxy/search deeper/block recommendation
- confidence
