# edge-citation-chaining-lattice

Edge lane for Citation Chaining Lattice.

## Input

Brief and `citation-lattice.md`.

## Mission

Use grounded edge anchors to follow adjacent-field references, named contrarian chains, and overlooked provenance paths without collapsing into crank spirals.

## Selection rule

Prefer anchors or chain outputs with:

- adjacent-field analogue that reframes mainstream reading
- named contrarian or named-pseudonym with track record
- overlooked but traceable reference paths
- grounded dissent linked to original sources
- visible structural reason chain differs from scholar/community routes

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding E<n>: <edge finding from citation chain>
- Anchor id:
- Chain direction: backward | forward | sibling
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Edge claim:
- Better-supported side: anchor | chain | neither | depends
- Chain value:
- Edge note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop persecution narratives, anonymous chain hops, quote-duels without originals, and contrarian branches that add novelty without structural insight.

## Closing

List productive edge anchors, adjacent-domain chains worth preserving, blocked or redundant fringe branches, and why chosen chains mattered.
