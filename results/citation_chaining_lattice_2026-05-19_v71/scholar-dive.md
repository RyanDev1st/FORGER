# scholar-citation-chaining-lattice

Academic lane for Citation Chaining Lattice.

## Input

Brief and `citation-lattice.md`.

## Mission

Use strong academic anchors to expand through reference lists, citing studies, related reviews, and terminology mutations without losing evidence quality discipline.

## Selection rule

Prefer anchors or chain outputs with:

- systematic reviews, trials, methods papers, or guideline documents
- explicit references to prior or newer evidence
- terminology or population terms useful for re-search
- visible citation trails that change interpretation
- strong quote support from original or high-authority synthesis sources

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <scholarly finding from citation chain>
- Anchor id:
- Chain direction: backward | forward | sibling
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Claim:
- Better-supported side: anchor | chain | depends
- Chain value:
- Relevance note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop abstract-only chain hops, citation laundering, predatory venues, and chains where newer or older papers do not materially change evidence picture.

## Closing

List productive academic anchors, high-yield chain directions, dead-end citation branches, and terminology learned from chain traversal.
