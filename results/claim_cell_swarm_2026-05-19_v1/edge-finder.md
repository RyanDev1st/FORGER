# edge-finder.md — Divergent Claim Cells

## Mission
Extract structurally unusual claim cells other lanes would miss. Edge means different discovery path, not weaker evidence.

## Sources
Priority order:
1. Adjacent-field analogues with transferable mechanisms.
2. Forgotten archives, old mailing lists, niche forums.
3. Non-English or regional practitioner sources.
4. Named-pseudonym experts with visible track record.
5. Substantive contrarians who engage strongest opposing claims.

## Search Procedure
1. Translate brief into at least three adjacent domains.
2. Search old terms, obsolete names, and dead paradigms.
3. Search non-English terms when topic has regional practice.
4. Search for failed theories that left useful mechanisms.
5. Stop at 5–8 accepted cells or documented exhaustion.

## Gate Per Cell
- Other-lane miss reason named.
- Verbatim quote captured.
- Claim type assigned: fact, method, warning, pattern, counterexample.
- Crank filter: no grandiosity, no unfalsifiable core, no selective evidence.
- Transfer mechanism explicit.
- Confidence defaults low unless independent corroboration exists.
