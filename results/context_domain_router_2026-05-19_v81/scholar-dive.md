# scholar-context-domain-router

Academic lane for Context Domain Router.

## Input

Brief and `domain-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Classify methodological and evidence claims by problem domain so research does not over-apply best practices to complex or changing systems.

## Selection rule

Prefer sources with:

- decision-context frameworks, methodological limits, and uncertainty discussion
- evidence on when expert analysis works or fails
- replication, causal-validity, or population-transfer warnings
- empirical data distinguishing stable from complex contexts
- stable identifiers and quotable text

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <scholar domain finding>
- Domain id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Academic subproblem:
- Domain: clear | complicated | complex | chaotic | disorder
- Why this domain:
- Evidence response needed:
- Wrong-response risk:
- Claim change:
- Confidence: high | medium | low
```

## Drop rules

Drop generic complexity claims, buzzword use, and sources that do not change method choice, evidence standard, or confidence tier.

## Closing

List domain-classified academic claims, method mismatches, complex claims needing probes, and unresolved disorder.
