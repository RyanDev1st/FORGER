# community-contradiction-matrix

Practitioner lane for Contradiction Reconciliation Matrix.

## Input

Brief and `contradiction-matrix.md`.

## Mission

Separate true field disagreement from version drift, environment mismatch, and anecdote inflation.

## Selection rule

Prefer conflicts with:

- tool-version or migration mismatch
- benchmark or environment mismatch
- first-hand versus second-hand retelling
- competing postmortems or maintainer guidance
- concrete implementation details and named practitioners

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding C<n>: <reconciled or unresolved practice conflict>
- Contradiction id:
- Conflict status: reconciled | partially reconciled | unresolved | false conflict
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Conflict axis:
- Better-supported side: A | B | neither | depends
- Practice implication:
- Reconciliation note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop conflicts built from anonymous anecdotes, snippet-only comparisons, or surface-level "X vs Y" debates with no operational detail.

## Closing

List reconciled field disputes, context-dependent practices, unresolved practitioner splits, and missing operational details.
