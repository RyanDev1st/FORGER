# edge-contradiction-matrix

Edge lane for Contradiction Reconciliation Matrix.

## Input

Brief and `contradiction-matrix.md`.

## Mission

Preserve grounded contrarian or paradigm conflicts without laundering weak dissent into equal status.

## Selection rule

Prefer conflicts with:

- meaningful challenge to mainstream framing
- adjacent-domain analogue that changes interpretation
- named contrarian with track record
- source-quality mismatch worth exposing
- visible reason disagreement persists

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding E<n>: <reconciled or unresolved edge conflict>
- Contradiction id:
- Conflict status: reconciled | partially reconciled | unresolved | false conflict
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Conflict axis:
- Better-supported side: A | B | neither | depends
- Edge value:
- Reconciliation note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop performative dissent, persecution narratives, quote-duels without originals, and conflicts where contrarian side adds no structural insight.

## Closing

List meaningful edge conflicts, false contrarian splits, unresolved paradigm tensions, and why they matter.
