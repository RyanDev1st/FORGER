# scholar-contradiction-matrix

Academic lane for Contradiction Reconciliation Matrix.

## Input

Brief and `contradiction-matrix.md`.

## Mission

Resolve or preserve scholarly conflicts by method, population, metric, timeframe, and evidence quality.

## Selection rule

Prefer conflicts with:

- differing study designs or evidence tiers
- null versus positive findings
- conflicting certainty or bias profiles
- population/context mismatch
- strong quote support from original or high-authority synthesis sources

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <reconciled or unresolved scholarly conflict>
- Contradiction id:
- Conflict status: reconciled | partially reconciled | unresolved | false conflict
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Conflict axis:
- Better-supported side: A | B | neither | depends
- Claim:
- Reconciliation note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop academic conflicts built from abstracts alone, mismatched claim levels, or source-quality differences too unclear to assess.

## Closing

List reconciled scholarly conflicts, unresolved evidence disputes, and variables driving disagreement.
