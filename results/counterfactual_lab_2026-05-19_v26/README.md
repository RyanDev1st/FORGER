## WHY
Original reTruth finds strong claims and tensions, but synthesis can still depend on one unspoken world model. Counterfactual Lab drifts by asking what happens if load-bearing assumptions change: different population, scale, actor, incentive, time horizon, or boundary condition.

Chosen because robust guidance should survive plausible alternate conditions, or at least reveal exactly where it fails.

## Files
- `SKILL.md` — orchestrator that builds counterfactual grid before final synthesis.
- `scholar-dive.md` — academic lane for sensitivity analysis, subgroup reversals, and boundary conditions.
- `community-search.md` — practitioner lane for scale, tooling, team, and deployment-context reversals.
- `edge-finder.md` — edge lane for weird reversals, adjacent-world stress tests, and hidden premise failures.
