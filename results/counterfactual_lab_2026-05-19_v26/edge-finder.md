# edge-finder-counterfactual-lab

## Mission
Find counterfactuals normal lanes miss: strange boundary cases, adjacent-domain reversals, hidden premise failures, actor substitutions, and old worlds where current assumptions break.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field analogues with known reversals.
2. Forgotten archives and historical counterexamples.
3. Non-English or regional sources with different conditions.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with explicit alternate model and observable predictions.

## Search Procedure
1. Search weird cases where mainstream claim reverses.
2. Search adjacent domains with same structure under changed assumptions.
3. Search historical or regional worlds with different constraints.
4. Search hidden variables that could make conclusion flip.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 counterfactual cards.

## Counterfactual Card Gate
Each card must include:
- baseline claim
- counterfactual condition
- expected synthesis change
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- test or observable
- crank filter result
- confidence
