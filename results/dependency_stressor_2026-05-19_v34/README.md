## WHY
Recommendations often fail not because evidence is wrong, but because hidden prerequisites do not hold. Dependency Stressor turns those prerequisites into first-class evidence objects. It asks what must already be true, what breaks if it is not true, and what cheap stress test can reveal fragility before action.
