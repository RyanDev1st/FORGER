# Gnosis — Double Diamond Grounding v76

## Status

v76. Search-first, browser-read, divergence-convergence gated.

## Scope

This variation turns reTruth into a double-diamond research loop: discover broadly, define the grounded problem, develop lane-divergent options, then deliver tested synthesis candidates.

## Pipeline

### Step 1 — Search first

Search external sources for research, design, implementation, and critique leads. Snippets only seed candidates.

### Step 2 — Browser-read candidates

Open candidate sources directly with `playwright-cli`. A lead cannot enter the diamond ledger without a visible page title and one verbatim quote.

### Step 3 — Build `diamond-ledger.md`

```markdown
### Diamond D<n>: <lead or claim cluster>
- Final URL:
- Source class: academic | practitioner | repo | dataset | forum | adjacent | blocked
- Browser status: opened | blocked | dead | mismatch
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Diamond phase: discover | define | develop | deliver
- Problem signal:
- Option signal:
- Test signal:
- Divergence value: low | medium | high
- Convergence readiness: not-ready | candidate | ready | reject
- Route hint: scholar | community | edge | re-search | synthesize
```

### Step 4 — Discover

Collect wide evidence before deciding the problem frame. Route method, implementation, failure, and adjacent-field leads into lanes without synthesizing.

### Step 5 — Define

Cluster evidence into candidate problem statements. Drop frames that lack source-backed user, domain, or mechanism evidence.

### Step 6 — Develop

Ask lanes for different answers to the defined problem. Scholar tests rigor, Community tests feasibility, Edge tests divergent inspiration.

### Step 7 — Deliver

Converge only after option evidence exists. Mark outputs as robust, testable prototype, exploratory spice, or rejected.

## Validation gates

- Search result snippets never count as evidence.
- Define phase cannot happen before discover evidence exists.
- Develop phase must preserve multiple option families.
- Deliver phase must include rejection of non-working options.
- Grounded creativity means inspiration after source mastery, not invention from vibes.

## Closing

Return final synthesis grouped by discovered problem, defined frame, developed options, rejected options, and deliverable next tests.
