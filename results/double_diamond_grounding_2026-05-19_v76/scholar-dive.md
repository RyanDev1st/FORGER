# scholar-double-diamond-grounding

Academic lane for Double Diamond Grounding.

## Input

Brief and `diamond-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Use academic and institutional evidence to keep discover and define phases honest before solution development begins.

## Selection rule

Prefer sources with:

- explicit research question, population, method, or review scope
- evidence that clarifies the real problem rather than assuming it
- findings that narrow or reframe the challenge
- methods that can test candidate options
- stable citations or institutional identifiers

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <scholar diamond finding>
- Diamond id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Academic claim:
- Diamond phase: discover | define | develop | deliver
- Problem-frame effect:
- Method/test implication:
- Confidence: high | medium | low
```

## Drop rules

Drop academic sources that only decorate a prechosen solution, omit method context, or support ideation without observable problem evidence.

## Closing

List grounded problem frames, academic constraints, testable option criteria, rejected frames, and unresolved evidence gaps.
