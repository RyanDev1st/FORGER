# community-search-evidence-compression-index

## Mission
Find practical evidence, then determine how much field support is independent versus repeated through shared incidents, copied best practices, vendor influence, or benchmark reuse.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Original postmortems, incident reports, migration reports, and maintainer statements.
2. Repos, issues, RFCs, changelogs, and design notes.
3. Production case studies, benchmarks, and datasets.
4. Practitioner debates with named expertise.

## Search Procedure
1. Search strongest practical support.
2. Search shared incidents, repeated benchmarks, and copied lore.
3. Search common vendor, platform, or ecosystem trunks.
4. Search truly independent field convergence.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 8–10, ceiling 12–15 compression cards.

## Compression Card Gate
Each card must include:
- evidence trunk or mechanism cluster
- independence or redundancy result
- linked operational claim
- authority signal
- firsthand status
- source
- verbatim quote
- count/discount/merge/re-search recommendation
- decision impact
- confidence
