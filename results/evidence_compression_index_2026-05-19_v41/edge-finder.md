# edge-finder-evidence-compression-index

## Mission
Find unconventional evidence, then test whether it adds real independence or just disguises redundancy through niche phrasing, old terminology, or contrarian branding.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
2. Non-English or regional sources.
3. Adjacent-field mechanism analogues.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with concrete source trails and observable claims.

## Search Procedure
1. Search edge evidence with possible independent value.
2. Search whether edge claims reduce to known mainstream trunks.
3. Search whether novelty is mechanism-deep or phrasing-deep.
4. Search where edge findings provide true non-redundant convergence.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 compression cards.

## Compression Card Gate
Each card must include:
- evidence trunk or mechanism cluster
- independence or redundancy result
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- count/discount/merge/re-search recommendation
- confidence
