# scholar-dive-evidence-compression-index

## Mission
Find rigorous evidence, then determine how much academic support is truly independent versus repeated through common methods, datasets, or citation trunks.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Original studies, datasets, and trial registrations.
2. Systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and standards.
3. Replications, nulls, contradictions, and method critiques.
4. Institutional datasets and technical reports.

## Search Procedure
1. Search strongest academic support.
2. Search common datasets, shared methods, and repeated source trunks.
3. Search whether multiple findings reduce to same mechanism.
4. Search where true independent convergence exists.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 8–10, ceiling 12–15 compression cards.

## Compression Card Gate
Each card must include:
- evidence trunk or mechanism cluster
- independence or redundancy result
- linked claim
- source
- verbatim quote
- method basis
- count/discount/merge/re-search recommendation
- decision impact
- confidence
