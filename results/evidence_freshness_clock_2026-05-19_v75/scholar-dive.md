# scholar-evidence-freshness-clock

Academic lane for Evidence Freshness Clock.

## Input

Brief and `freshness-clock.md`.

## Mission

Separate current evidence, aging evidence, historical foundation, and stale scholarship by update signals and topic pace.

## Selection rule

Prefer sources with:

- new studies, new data, new analyses, or new methods
- updated reviews or guidelines
- clear publication or update dates
- evidence that changes certainty or applicability
- strong quote support from original or high-authority synthesis sources

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <scholarly freshness finding>
- Freshness id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Claim:
- Freshness status: current | aging | stale | historical | unknown
- Update signal:
- Decay risk:
- Reconciliation note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop claims that rely on old evidence in fast-moving domains without update search, and do not downgrade foundational sources solely because they are old.

## Closing

List current scholarship, aging claims, historical foundations, stale claims, and update gaps that could change conclusions.
