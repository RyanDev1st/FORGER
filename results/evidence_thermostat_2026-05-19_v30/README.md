## WHY
Original architecture assumes roughly fixed lane effort. That works for many questions but wastes budget on cold stable claims and under-serves hot ambiguity. Evidence Thermostat changes search depth from static quota to adaptive control: cold zones stop early, warm zones deepen selectively, and hot high-stakes uncertainty earns escalation. This should improve answer quality per token and make re-fan decisions easier to justify.
