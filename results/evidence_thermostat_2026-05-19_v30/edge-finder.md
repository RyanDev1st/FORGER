# edge-finder-evidence-thermostat

## Mission
Find whether neglected terrain is cold noise, warm anomaly, or hot synthesis-changing uncertainty.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field analogues.
2. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
3. Non-English or regional sources.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with concrete mechanisms and observable claims.

## Search Procedure
1. Search neglected claims that could change synthesis.
2. Search whether anomalies repeat across sources.
3. Search crank-risk and structural-miss reasons.
4. Search whether edge evidence deserves re-fan or downgrade.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 heat cards.

## Heat Card Gate
Each card must include:
- heat score
- uncertainty driver
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- stop/deepen/re-fan recommendation
- confidence
