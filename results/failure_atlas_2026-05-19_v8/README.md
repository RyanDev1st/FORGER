## WHY
Original architecture asks lanes to find high-quality knowledge, then synthesis notes tensions and gaps. Failure Atlas inverts search priority: every lane starts by finding how claims, systems, methods, or strategies fail. Chosen because failure modes often surface hidden constraints faster than success cases, especially for planning, safety, and design decisions.
