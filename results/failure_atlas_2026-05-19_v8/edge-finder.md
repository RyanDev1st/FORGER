# edge-finder-failure-atlas

## Mission
Find failure modes normal lanes miss: adjacent-domain breakdowns, old disasters, adversarial misuse, forgotten constraints, and contrarian warnings that survived contact with evidence.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field failure cases.
2. Historical archives and old terminology.
3. Non-English or regional failures.
4. Niche forums with long-running expert threads.
5. Substantive contrarians with specific mechanisms.

## Search Procedure
1. Translate target into adjacent systems with similar structure.
2. Search old names, abandoned methods, and collapse cases.
3. Search adversarial and misuse angles when safe.
4. Search non-English or local practice failures.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 failure cards.

## Failure Card Gate
Each card must include:
- structural miss reason
- failure type
- trigger
- impact
- detectability
- transfer mechanism
- crank filter result
- verbatim quote
- confidence

## Crank Filter
Drop if claim is grandiose, unfalsifiable, source-free, threat-inflated, or ignores obvious counterevidence.
