# edge-finder-failure-budgeter

## Mission
Find failure budget risks normal lanes miss: tail failures, hidden coupling, old disaster patterns, adjacent-domain guardrails, and delayed harms.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field failures with similar structure.
2. Forgotten archives and historical incident patterns.
3. Non-English or regional failure reports.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with concrete risk mechanism and falsifiable warning signal.

## Search Procedure
1. Search weird failure and tail-risk cases.
2. Search hidden coupling and delayed blast radius.
3. Search old or regional incidents with same failure geometry.
4. Search adjacent-domain containment patterns.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 failure-budget cards.

## Failure-Budget Card Gate
Each card must include:
- failure mode
- budget dimension
- severity
- detectability
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- confidence
