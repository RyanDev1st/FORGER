# scholar-incentive-bias-ledger

Academic lane for Incentive Bias Ledger.

## Input

Brief and `incentive-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Assess academic evidence through disclosed and plausible incentives without confusing conflict presence with automatic invalidity.

## Selection rule

Prefer sources with:

- funding, sponsor, author relationship, or data-access information
- plausible pathway from incentive to design, conduct, analysis, reporting, omission, or framing
- independent replication or synthesis checks
- missing-results or publication-bias relevance
- strong quote support from original or high-authority synthesis sources

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <scholarly incentive finding>
- Incentive id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Claim:
- Incentive vector:
- Bias pathway:
- Weight effect: upgrade | neutral | downgrade | exclude
- Reconciliation note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop generic ad hominem conflict claims, undisclosed accusations without source support, and incentives with no plausible pathway to evidence distortion.

## Closing

List academic claims retained, downgraded, excluded, and disclosure gaps that matter to synthesis confidence.
