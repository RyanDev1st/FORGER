# gnosis-incentive-gradient

## Invocation
Use when recommendation adoption changes actor behavior: incentives, metrics, rules, pricing, ranking, enforcement, subsidies, audits, benchmarks, or social status.

## Pipeline

### Step 1 — Parse Incentive Frame
Extract:
- central recommendation
- actors affected
- current incentive structure
- changed incentive after adoption
- likely strategic response
- unintended behavior risk
- monitoring signal

### Step 2 — Spawn Lanes
Spawn Scholar, Community, and Edge in parallel. Each lane emits incentive cards plus findings.

### Step 3 — Validate
Run V1–V8:
- V1 file exists and non-empty.
- V2 at least 5 incentive cards or pivot log.
- V3 no top-level refusal.
- V4 every card has source and verbatim quote.
- V5 every card names actor and incentive shift.
- V6 every card names predicted behavioral adaptation.
- V7 every card recommends preserve, redesign, monitor, or reject.
- V8 closing block present.

### Step 4 — Audit
Check URLs, quote matches, actor specificity, and Edge redundancy. Preserve failures as downgrade flags.

### Step 5 — Build Incentive Gradient
Classify cards into:
- aligned adaptation
- gaming risk
- moral hazard
- adverse selection
- metric capture
- compliance theater
- displacement effect
- stabilizing feedback

### Step 6 — Synthesize
Return:
- recommendation after incentive response
- actors likely to adapt
- beneficial adaptations to preserve
- harmful adaptations to redesign against
- monitoring signals and guardrails

### Step 7 — Re-fan
Re-fan only when behavioral adaptation could invert the recommendation. One round max unless user escalates.

## Output
Return:
1. Incentive gradient map.
2. Preserve/redesign/monitor/reject decisions.
3. Current synthesis after adaptation.
4. Gaming and moral-hazard hot spots.
5. Monitoring signals.
6. Evidence appendix.

## Constraints
- No static recommendation when actors can strategically adapt.
- No incentive claim without actor, shift, and adaptation.
- Maximum four concurrent threads.
- Preserve raw evidence appendices.
