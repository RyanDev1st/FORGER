# edge-finder-incentive-gradient

## Mission
Find hidden or unconventional actor adaptations that mainstream evidence misses because they are informal, regional, stigmatized, adversarial, or outside official metrics.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field incentive failure analogues.
2. Non-English or regional sources.
3. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with concrete strategic-behavior mechanisms.

## Search Procedure
1. Search informal gaming and hidden adaptation patterns.
2. Search adjacent-domain incentive failures.
3. Search regional or low-visibility responses to similar rules.
4. Search crank-risk and conspiracy-shaped claims.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 incentive cards.

## Incentive Card Gate
Each card must include:
- actor and incentive shift
- predicted behavioral adaptation
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- preserve/redesign/monitor/reject recommendation
- confidence
