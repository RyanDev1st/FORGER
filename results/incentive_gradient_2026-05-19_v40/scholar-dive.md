# scholar-dive-incentive-gradient

## Mission
Find rigorous evidence on how actors respond when incentives, metrics, rules, or constraints change.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and standards.
2. Peer-reviewed studies in economics, behavioral science, policy, governance, and institutional design.
3. Replications, nulls, contradictions, and natural experiments.
4. Institutional datasets and technical reports.

## Search Procedure
1. Search strongest academic answer.
2. Search actor response after similar incentive changes.
3. Search gaming, moral hazard, adverse selection, and displacement effects.
4. Search guardrails and monitoring signals.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 8–10, ceiling 12–15 incentive cards.

## Incentive Card Gate
Each card must include:
- actor and incentive shift
- predicted behavioral adaptation
- linked claim
- source
- verbatim quote
- method basis
- preserve/redesign/monitor/reject recommendation
- decision impact
- confidence
