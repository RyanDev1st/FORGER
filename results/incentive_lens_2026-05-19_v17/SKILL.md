# gnosis-incentive-lens

## Invocation
Use when topic involves vendors, institutions, maintainers, regulators, markets, movements, or any setting where actor incentives may distort visible truth.

## Core Shift
Every finding must carry incentive context. Synthesis weighs not only evidence quality, but motive structure and pressure asymmetry.

## Pipeline

### Step 1 — Parse Brief
Extract:
- target claim or decision
- stakeholder groups
- suspected incentive conflicts
- harm level
- visibility asymmetry
- decision stakes

### Step 2 — Create Workspace
Create:
- `scholar-dive.md`
- `community-search.md`
- `edge-finder.md`
- `incentive-map.md`
- `misalignment-register.md`

### Step 3 — Spawn Lanes
Spawn three isolated lanes. Each lane emits findings with incentive cards.

### Step 4 — Validate
Run V1–V8:
- V1 file exists and non-empty.
- V2 at least 5 incentive cards or pivot log.
- V3 no top-level refusal.
- V4 every card has verbatim quote.
- V5 every card names actor and incentive.
- V6 every card states distortion risk.
- V7 every card states who pays cost if wrong.
- V8 closing block present.

### Step 5 — Audit
Run URL, quote, and Edge redundancy checks. Preserve failures as flags.

### Step 6 — Build Incentive Map
Classify by:
- commercial incentive
- institutional incentive
- career incentive
- ideological incentive
- coordination incentive
- liability or regulation incentive
- prestige or status incentive

### Step 7 — Synthesize
Return:
- evidence robust even under incentives
- evidence likely bent by incentives
- misalignment hot spots
- stakeholder-sensitive recommendations

### Step 8 — Re-fan
If one actor group dominates uncertainty, re-fan one lane with narrow incentive brief. One round max.

## Output
Return:
1. Incentive map.
2. Misalignment register.
3. Claims robust to motive distortion.
4. Claims suspect under pressure.
5. Monitoring cues for incentive shifts.

## Constraints
- No motive speculation without evidence cues.
- No ad hominem substitution for evidence.
- Maximum four concurrent threads.
- Preserve raw evidence appendices.
