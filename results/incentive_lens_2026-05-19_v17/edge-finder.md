# edge-finder-incentive-lens

## Mission
Find incentive structures normal lanes miss: taboo pressures, unspoken coordination incentives, regional motive shifts, and hidden cost externalization.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-domain analogues of incentive failure.
2. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
3. Non-English or regional sources.
4. Niche expert forums.
5. Substantive contrarians with specific mechanisms.

## Search Procedure
1. Search for hidden or taboo incentive language.
2. Search regional or historical pressure shifts.
3. Search adjacent systems with similar actor geometry.
4. Search externalized-cost stories and incentive traps.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 cards.

## Incentive Card Gate
Each card must include:
- actor
- incentive type
- linked claim
- distortion risk
- who pays cost if wrong
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- confidence
