# Variation Pipeline — Incentive Lens

Parent: ARCHITECTURE.md

## Status
Created v17 variation centered on motive structure and stakeholder misalignment.

## Pipeline
1. Parse brief into claim, actors, and stakes.
2. Spawn three isolated lanes.
3. Force each lane to emit incentive cards.
4. Validate actor, incentive type, distortion risk, cost bearer, quote, and source.
5. Audit links, quotes, and redundancy.
6. Build incentive map and misalignment register.
7. Distinguish evidence robust to incentives from evidence likely bent by them.
8. Re-fan one dominant uncertain actor group if needed.

## WHY
Incentive Lens improves reTruth when bias is structural, not merely rhetorical. It helps explain why weak claims persist, why strong claims stay hidden, and where recommendations may fail due to actor misalignment.

## Difference From Original
Original architecture includes bias gates. Incentive Lens moves beyond source bias into system-level motive analysis.

## Expected Benefit
- Better stakeholder realism.
- Stronger misalignment detection.
- Better reading of strategic narratives.
- More useful risk monitoring.

## Tradeoff
Easy to over-interpret motives. Mitigation: every incentive claim still needs source-linked evidence cue.
