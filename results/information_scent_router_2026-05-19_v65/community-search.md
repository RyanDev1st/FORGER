# community-scent-router

Practitioner lane for Information Scent Router.

## Input

Brief and `scent-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Spend field-research effort on leads with high action scent: clear implementation terms, named practitioners, failure details, concrete artifacts.

## Selection rule

Prefer leads with:

- maintainer or named practitioner cue
- postmortem/migration/failure language
- repo, dataset, benchmark, or docs artifact
- low extraction cost

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding C<n>: <practice claim>
- Scent lead id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Why selected: <action cue|failure cue|artifact cue>
- Practice implication:
- Adoption risk:
- Search lesson: <query cue to reuse>
```

## Drop rules

Drop high-clickbait scent, vendor CTAs, generic tutorials, and pages where visible text does not expose operational detail.

## Closing

List strongest action-scent cues, misleading practitioner leads, and missing field evidence.
