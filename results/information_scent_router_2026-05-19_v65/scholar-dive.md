# scholar-scent-router

Academic lane for Information Scent Router.

## Input

Brief and `scent-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Spend time where method signal and corroboration potential are strongest.

## Selection rule

Prefer leads with:

- strong authority cues
- clear method vocabulary
- easy quote extraction
- high corroboration potential

Avoid expensive weak-scent papers unless topic is under-sourced.

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <method or evidence claim>
- Scent lead id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Why selected: <authority|method|corroboration>
- Claim:
- Confidence:
- Search lesson: <term or cue to reuse>
```

## Drop rules

Drop academic leads with weak method cue, no clear quote, or no corroboration path unless explicitly logged as fragile.

## Closing

List best academic scent cues, wasted academic clicks, and what stronger search wording emerged.
