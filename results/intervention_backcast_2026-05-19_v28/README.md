## WHY
Original reTruth is strong at explaining topics, but action questions need reverse engineering from desired outcome to controllable levers. Intervention Backcast drifts by starting with target outcome, tracing backward through intermediate states, then ranking interventions by controllability and evidence strength.

Chosen because many users do not only want to know what is true; they want to know what to change first, why that lever should move the outcome, and what failure paths to watch.

## Files
- `SKILL.md` — orchestrator that builds intervention backcast chains.
- `scholar-dive.md` — academic lane for intervention studies, mechanisms, mediators, and adverse effects.
- `community-search.md` — practitioner lane for field-tested levers, rollout bottlenecks, and operational traps.
- `edge-finder.md` — edge lane for neglected levers, adjacent-domain analogues, and hidden guardrails.
- `variation-pipeline.md` — pipeline, expected gains, risks, and next variations.
