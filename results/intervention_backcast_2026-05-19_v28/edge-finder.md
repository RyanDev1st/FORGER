# edge-finder-intervention-backcast

## Mission
Find intervention chains normal lanes miss: indirect levers, adjacent-domain analogues, overlooked bottlenecks, old playbooks, and hidden failure guardrails.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field interventions with similar outcome geometry.
2. Forgotten archives and historical playbooks.
3. Non-English or regional implementation reports.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with concrete mechanism and observable outcome.

## Search Procedure
1. Search indirect routes to target outcome.
2. Search adjacent domains where same lever moved similar outcome.
3. Search old or regional interventions that mainstream terms miss.
4. Search failure modes that make obvious interventions backfire.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 intervention cards.

## Intervention Card Gate
Each card must include:
- intervention
- intermediate state
- target outcome
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- controllability
- crank filter result
- confidence
