# scholar-dive-intervention-friction-audit

## Mission
Find rigorous evidence showing whether intervention effects survive implementation friction. Prioritize implementation science, adoption studies, adherence research, cost-effectiveness work, and studies with real-world fidelity measures.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Implementation science reviews, pragmatic trials, and field experiments.
2. Cost-effectiveness studies with setup, training, operating, or maintenance costs.
3. Studies reporting adherence, fidelity, dropout, compliance, or uptake.
4. Institutional reports that compare pilot results against scaled deployment.

## Search Procedure
1. Search claimed intervention benefit.
2. Search implementation fidelity, adherence, and uptake limits.
3. Search operational, training, compliance, and maintenance burden.
4. Search scaled-deployment evidence versus pilot evidence.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 8–10, ceiling 12–15 friction cards.

## Friction Card Gate
Each card must include:
- friction source
- benefit erosion mechanism
- theoretical benefit
- friction-adjusted benefit
- adopt/redesign/stage/defer/reject recommendation
- source
- verbatim quote
- applicability limit
- measurement needed
- decision impact
- confidence
