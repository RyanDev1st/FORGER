# edge-need-to-knowledge-broker

Edge lane for Need-to-Knowledge Broker.

## Input

Brief and `ntk-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Find unconventional transfer paths, overlooked stakeholder groups, adjacent inventions, and neglected existing knowledge without drifting into need-free novelty.

## Selection rule

Prefer sources with:

- adjacent domains that solved a similar need
- forgotten patents, dead prototypes, or revived methods with clear provenance
- non-obvious stakeholder constraints
- community adoption paths outside institutions or vendors
- mechanisms that explain transfer failure or success

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding E<n>: <edge NtK finding>
- NtK id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Edge claim:
- NtK phase: need | existing-knowledge | invention | development | implementation | impact
- Unusual stakeholder or transfer path:
- Grounded need link:
- Test/translation hook:
```

## Drop rules

Drop novelty without need evidence, invention-first contrarianism, and transfer fantasies with no stakeholder path.

## Closing

List neglected knowledge, unusual stakeholders, adjacent transfer mechanisms, dead-end inventions, and edge candidates worth translating.
