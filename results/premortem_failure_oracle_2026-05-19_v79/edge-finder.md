# edge-premortem-failure-oracle

Edge lane for Premortem Failure Oracle.

## Input

Brief and `premortem-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Find weird, neglected, or socially uncomfortable reasons the synthesis might fail, without letting contrarianism become noise.

## Selection rule

Prefer sources with:

- ignored dissent that later proved useful
- adjacent-domain failures with matching mechanism
- unusual stakeholder harms or incentive traps
- dead prototypes that reveal hidden constraints
- named or track-record pseudonymous practitioners

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding E<n>: <edge premortem finding>
- Premortem id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Edge failure risk:
- Why normal lanes may miss it:
- Failure mechanism:
- Grounding evidence:
- Mitigation or kill criterion:
```

## Drop rules

Drop paranoia, persecution narratives, unfalsifiable warnings, and novelty risks with no mechanism or kill criterion.

## Closing

List blind spots, uncomfortable dissent, weird failure paths, kill criteria, and risks that should remain visible in synthesis.
