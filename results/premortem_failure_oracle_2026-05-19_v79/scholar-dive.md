# scholar-premortem-failure-oracle

Academic lane for Premortem Failure Oracle.

## Input

Brief and `premortem-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Anticipate how the final synthesis could fail methodologically, then search evidence for those failure causes.

## Selection rule

Prefer sources with:

- replication failures, negative results, limitations, or contradictory reviews
- measurement, population, or causal-validity warnings
- evidence on false assumptions or bad metrics
- formal risk, bias, or uncertainty discussion
- stable identifiers and quotable text

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <scholar premortem finding>
- Premortem id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Academic failure risk:
- Failure cause:
- Evidence supports risk:
- Evidence weakens risk:
- Mitigation:
- Confidence: high | medium | low
```

## Drop rules

Drop vague limitations, ungrounded skepticism, and failure claims not tied to method, population, measurement, or evidence quality.

## Closing

List high-impact academic failure modes, mitigated risks, downgraded claims, and evidence gaps needing re-search.
