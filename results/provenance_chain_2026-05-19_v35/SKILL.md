# gnosis-provenance-chain

## Invocation
Use when repeated claims may be downstream echoes, mutated summaries, citation laundering, or meme-like transmission instead of independent evidence.

## Pipeline

### Step 1 — Parse Provenance Frame
Extract:
- central claim
- likely origin type
- repeated downstream forms
- suspected relay layers
- mutation risk
- evidence stakes
- need for origin recovery

### Step 2 — Spawn Lanes
Spawn Scholar, Community, and Edge in parallel. Each lane emits provenance cards plus findings.

### Step 3 — Validate
Run V1–V8:
- V1 file exists and non-empty.
- V2 at least 5 provenance cards or pivot log.
- V3 no top-level refusal.
- V4 every card has source and verbatim quote.
- V5 every card names claim stage.
- V6 every card names mutation or fidelity risk.
- V7 every card recommends trust, trace deeper, downgrade, or discard.
- V8 closing block present.

### Step 4 — Audit
Check URLs, quote matches, chronology, and Edge redundancy. Preserve failures as downgrade flags.

### Step 5 — Build Provenance Map
Classify cards into:
- origin source
- early relay
- stable restatement
- mutated summary
- citation laundering
- orphaned claim
- fidelity-preserving chain
- broken provenance

### Step 6 — Synthesize
Return:
- origin confidence
- strongest direct evidence
- claims safe to trust
- claims degraded by relay or mutation
- deepest trace still worth paying for

### Step 7 — Re-fan
Re-fan only when origin uncertainty changes recommendation strength. One round max unless user escalates.

## Output
Return:
1. Provenance map.
2. Trust/trace deeper/downgrade/discard decisions.
3. Current synthesis.
4. Mutation hot spots.
5. Origin recovery targets.
6. Evidence appendix.

## Constraints
- No consensus count inflation from echoed claims.
- No downstream restatement treated as origin evidence.
- Maximum four concurrent threads.
- Preserve raw evidence appendices.
