# edge-finder-provenance-chain

## Mission
Find neglected origin trails, old forms, regional variants, and mutation paths that reveal whether a claim is older, narrower, stronger, or weaker than current retellings.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
2. Non-English or regional sources.
3. Adjacent-field origin analogues.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with concrete source trails and observable claims.

## Search Procedure
1. Search old terminology and earliest known claim variants.
2. Search regional or non-English origin trails.
3. Search mutation paths across adjacent fields.
4. Search crank-risk, forged provenance, and circular citation risk.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 provenance cards.

## Provenance Card Gate
Each card must include:
- claim stage
- mutation or fidelity risk
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- chain relation
- crank filter result
- trust/trace deeper/downgrade/discard recommendation
- confidence
