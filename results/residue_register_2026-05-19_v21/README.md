## WHY
Original reTruth is strong at finding claims, tensions, and synthesis, but good answers can still over-close: weak silence becomes absence, failed searches vanish, and unresolved definitions get smoothed into prose. Residue Register improves result quality by making uncertainty durable, ranked, and actionable.

This drift is useful when user needs to know not only what is true, but what remains unproven, inaccessible, stale, or decision-critical.
