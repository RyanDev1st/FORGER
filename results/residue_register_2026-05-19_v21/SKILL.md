# gnosis-residue-register

## Invocation
Use when premature closure is dangerous: strategic research, disputed topics, high-stakes decisions, or domains where missing evidence matters as much as found evidence.

## Pipeline

### Step 1 — Parse Closure Risk
Extract:
- central question
- likely answer-shapes
- known uncertainty tolerance
- decision pressure
- evidence gaps that would change action
- categories of residue to track

### Step 2 — Spawn Lanes
Spawn Scholar, Community, and Edge in parallel. Each lane emits residue cards plus supported findings.

### Step 3 — Validate
Run V1–V8:
- V1 file exists and non-empty.
- V2 at least 5 residue cards or pivot log.
- V3 no top-level refusal.
- V4 every card has verbatim quote or explicit failed-source trail.
- V5 every card has residue type.
- V6 every card has action relevance.
- V7 every card has resolution route.
- V8 closing block present.

### Step 4 — Audit
Check URLs, quote matches, failed-source trails, and Edge redundancy. Preserve audit failures as residue, not deletion.

### Step 5 — Build Residue Register
Classify cards into:
- missing evidence
- ambiguous definition
- source silence
- contradiction unresolved
- stale evidence
- inaccessible evidence
- method limitation
- decision dependency

### Step 6 — Synthesize
Return:
- answerable claims
- unresolved residues ranked by decision impact
- claims that should be downgraded because of residue
- safest provisional answer
- what evidence would close the register fastest

### Step 7 — Re-fan
If one residue item blocks action-quality synthesis, re-fan one lane with narrow brief. One round max.

## Output
Return:
1. Provisional answer.
2. Residue register.
3. Downgraded claims.
4. Fastest closure tests.
5. Evidence appendix.

## Constraints
- No confident conclusion without named residue scan.
- Do not hide failed searches; classify them.
- Maximum four concurrent threads.
- Preserve raw evidence appendices.
