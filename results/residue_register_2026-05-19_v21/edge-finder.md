# edge-finder-residue-register

## Mission
Find residue normal lanes miss: forgotten failures, old terminology, regional evidence holes, adjacent-domain unknowns, and questions made invisible by mainstream framing.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field analogues with similar uncertainty shape.
2. Forgotten archives and older terminology.
3. Non-English or regional sources.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist blogs.
5. Contrarian sources with concrete unresolved evidence, not vibe.

## Search Procedure
1. Search old names and adjacent framings for missing evidence.
2. Search who would have observed the issue but stayed silent.
3. Search regional reversals and weird failures.
4. Search claims where absence of evidence is structurally meaningful.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 residue cards.

## Residue Card Gate
Each card must include:
- residue type
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- source
- verbatim quote or failed-source trail
- why unresolved
- transfer relevance
- crank filter result
- resolution route
- confidence
