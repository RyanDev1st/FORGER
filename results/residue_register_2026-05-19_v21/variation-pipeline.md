# Variation Pipeline — Residue Register

Parent: ARCHITECTURE.md

## Status
Created v21 variation centered on unresolved residue, failed search trails, and answer debt.

## Pipeline
1. Parse brief into central question, likely answer-shapes, uncertainty tolerance, decision pressure, and residue categories.
2. Spawn three isolated lanes.
3. Force each lane to emit residue cards alongside supported findings.
4. Validate residue type, linked claim, quote or failed-source trail, why unresolved, decision impact, and resolution route.
5. Audit URLs, quote matches, failed-source trails, and Edge redundancy.
6. Build residue register:
   - missing evidence
   - ambiguous definition
   - source silence
   - contradiction unresolved
   - stale evidence
   - inaccessible evidence
   - method limitation
   - decision dependency
7. Synthesize provisional answer with residue-weighted downgrades.
8. Re-fan one blocking residue item if needed.

## WHY
Residue Register improves reTruth by preventing polished synthesis from hiding unresolved answer debt. It keeps uncertainty operational: each gap has a type, linked claim, decision impact, and resolution route.

## Difference From Original
Original architecture reports gaps and tensions. Residue Register makes gaps the main ledger and uses them to downgrade claims, prioritize next research, and prevent false closure.

## Expected Benefit
- More honest provisional answers.
- Better handling of failed searches and source silence.
- Clearer next evidence targets.
- Less overconfident synthesis when evidence terrain is incomplete.

## Tradeoff
More output devoted to uncertainty. Best for high-stakes, ambiguous, or research-heavy questions; less useful for simple lookup tasks.

## Main Risk
Can overweight unknowns and make synthesis feel less decisive. Mitigation: rank residue by decision impact, not by quantity.

## Next Variation Ideas
1. Translation Engine: map same claim across lane vocabularies.
2. Temporal Decay: make evidence age and update cadence central.
3. Stakeholder Prism: synthesize by affected party, not by claim.
