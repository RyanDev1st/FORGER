# edge-finder-reversal-sentinel

## Mission
Find edge signals that warned before consensus reversed, or failed to reverse despite confident claims, while filtering crank narratives.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field reversals with similar mechanism.
2. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
3. Non-English or regional sources where flip happened earlier.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with dated claims and observable track record.

## Search Procedure
1. Search neglected early warnings that preceded reversal.
2. Search failed reversal predictions and why they failed.
3. Search regional or adjacent-domain flips not yet generalized.
4. Search crank-risk and mechanism transfer limits.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 reversal cards.

## Reversal Card Gate
Each card must include:
- reversal direction
- reversal mechanism
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- chronology
- crank filter result
- trust/hedge/test/reject recommendation
- confidence
