# edge-finder-robustness-ladder

## Mission
Find high-variance evidence and grade whether it survives enough stress classes to be hypothesis, warning, or action-grade signal.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field analogues with observable transfer.
2. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
3. Non-English or regional sources.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with concrete mechanisms and track record.

## Search Procedure
1. Search edge claims with potential synthesis value.
2. Search specificity, track record, and transfer stress tests.
3. Search crank-filter failures and non-redundancy checks.
4. Search minimum evidence needed to promote from anomaly to useful signal.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 robustness cards.

## Robustness Card Gate
Each card must include:
- stress class
- pass/partial/fail result
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- current tier
- promote/hold/demote/discard recommendation
- confidence
