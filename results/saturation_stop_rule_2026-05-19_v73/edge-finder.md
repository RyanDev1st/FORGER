# edge-saturation-stop-rule

Edge lane for Saturation Stop Rule.

## Input

Brief and `saturation-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Decide whether edge search has yielded enough structurally divergent insight or whether scarcity, blocked archives, or crank-filter attrition require preserving an edge gap.

## Selection rule

Prefer rounds with:

- low-volume but high-structure adjacent-domain insight
- named contrarian or named-pseudonym evidence
- repeated crank-filter drops that justify stopping a branch
- blocked archives or dead boards that explain scarcity
- source-quality mismatch worth surfacing

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding E<n>: <edge saturation finding>
- Saturation round id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Edge claim:
- Saturation status: reached | not reached | blocked | uncertain
- Stop implication:
- Edge note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop stop claims that protect weak dissent, persecution narratives, anonymous anecdotes, or novelty-only routes with no structural value.

## Closing

List edge stop decisions, preserved scarcity gaps, blocked archive routes, and why continued edge search would or would not matter.
