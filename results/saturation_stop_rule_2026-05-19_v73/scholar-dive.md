# scholar-saturation-stop-rule

Academic lane for Saturation Stop Rule.

## Input

Brief and `saturation-ledger.md`.

## Mission

Decide whether academic search has reached defensible saturation or whether missed-study risk, scarce evidence, or unresolved method gaps require continued search or preserved uncertainty.

## Selection rule

Prefer rounds with:

- evidence of new relevant studies or reviews
- clear redundancy across high-quality sources
- missed-study risk or recall concern
- scarce evidence where stopping needs caution
- strong quote support from original or high-authority synthesis sources

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <scholarly saturation finding>
- Saturation round id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Claim:
- Saturation status: reached | not reached | blocked | uncertain
- Stop implication:
- Reconciliation note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop saturation claims based only on quota, search fatigue, abstract-only evidence, or untested assumption that no more studies exist.

## Closing

List academic stop decisions, continuing-search triggers, missed-source risks, and unresolved scholarly gaps.
