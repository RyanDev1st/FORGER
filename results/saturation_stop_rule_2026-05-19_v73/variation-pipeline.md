# Variation Pipeline — Saturation Stop Rule v73

## Status

v73. Search-first, browser-read, stop-rule explicit.

## Scope

This variation adds a saturation ledger so search stops only when marginal yield, redundancy, precision collapse, access limits, and missed-source risk are visible.

## Pipeline

1. Parse topic breadth, harm domain, source density, and missed-source cost.
2. Search broad lead pool across supportive, critical, null, failure, and edge routes.
3. Open candidates directly with `playwright-cli`.
4. Build `saturation-ledger.md` by search round.
5. Track sources opened, included findings, new relevant records, redundancy, blocked/dead records, precision signal, scarcity caution, missed-source risk, and stop decision.
6. Continue, pivot, stop, or preserve gap based on ledger state.
7. Apply scarcity override for sparse, contested, or high-harm domains.
8. Route saturation judgments to scholar, community, and edge lanes.
9. Synthesize findings with explicit stop decisions and remaining missed-source risks.

## Audit checks

- No lane stops only because floor or ceiling was reached.
- Stop decision cites yield, redundancy, precision, blocked access, or missed-source risk.
- Scarce evidence triggers caution rather than premature closure.
- Preserved gaps are named when saturation was not reached.
