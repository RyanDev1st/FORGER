# community-search-provenance-spine

Practitioner lane for Search Provenance Spine.

## Input

Brief and `provenance-spine.md`.

## Mission

Preserve auditable practitioner search path so docs, repos, benchmarks, and maintainer evidence stay tied to concrete routing and exclusion choices.

## Selection rule

Prefer provenance steps with:

- maintainer or repo trail changes
- benchmark or environment reroutes
- exclusion of marketing or anonymous anecdotes
- concrete interface or access constraints
- named practitioners and operational detail

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding C<n>: <practice finding with provenance>
- Provenance step id:
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Practice claim:
- Search-path effect:
- Better-supported side: source | route | depends
- Repro note:
```

## Drop rules

Drop provenance notes that only record trivial clicks, surface-level ranking, or unlinked anecdotes with no operational effect on evidence path.

## Closing

List practitioner findings, route-defining provenance steps, dead or blocked applied sources, and remaining reproducibility gaps.
