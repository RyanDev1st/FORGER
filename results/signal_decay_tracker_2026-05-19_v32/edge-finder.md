# edge-finder-signal-decay-tracker

## Mission
Find whether neglected evidence is decayed noise, forgotten evergreen signal, or early warning of a new shift.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field analogues across time periods.
2. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
3. Non-English or regional sources with different update cycles.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with dated predictions and observable track record.

## Search Procedure
1. Search old evidence that may still outperform current consensus.
2. Search fresh edge signals not yet absorbed by mainstream sources.
3. Search repeated stale claims and origin drift.
4. Search crank-risk, update failure, and transfer limits.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 decay cards.

## Decay Card Gate
Each card must include:
- evidence date or era
- decay driver
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- keep/discount/refresh/retire recommendation
- confidence
