# edge-finder-signal-refinery

## Mission
Find unusual signals normal lanes miss, then calibrate them harshly.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field mechanisms.
2. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
3. Non-English or regional practice.
4. Niche experts with track record.
5. Substantive contrarians with mechanisms.

## Search Procedure
1. Search adjacent mechanisms.
2. Search old or regional signal sources.
3. Search counterexamples and anomalies.
4. Search independent corroboration.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 packets.

## Signal Packet Gate
Each packet must include:
- signal direction: supports, weakens, narrows, unknown
- signal strength: strong, moderate, weak
- reliability: high, medium, low
- actionability: high, medium, low
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- noise flag
