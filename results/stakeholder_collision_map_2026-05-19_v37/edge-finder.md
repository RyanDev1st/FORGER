# edge-finder-stakeholder-collision-map

## Mission
Find stakeholder collisions mainstream sources miss because affected groups are low-status, regional, informal, non-English, or outside standard metrics.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Non-English or regional sources from affected groups.
2. Forgotten archives and old terminology.
3. Adjacent-field stakeholder conflict analogues.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with concrete mechanisms and affected-party evidence.

## Search Procedure
1. Search overlooked affected groups.
2. Search hidden externalities and informal burden shifts.
3. Search regional or adjacent-domain conflict analogues.
4. Search crank-risk and representation quality.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 collision cards.

## Collision Card Gate
Each card must include:
- stakeholder pair or group
- collision mechanism
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- crank filter result
- align/compensate/constrain/split recommendation
- confidence
