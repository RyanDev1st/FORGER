# scholar-dive-stakeholder-collision-map

## Mission
Find rigorous evidence about how different groups experience benefits, harms, incentives, and outcome definitions.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and standards with subgroup analysis.
2. Peer-reviewed distributional, equity, economic, and institutional studies.
3. Replications, nulls, contradictions, and critiques of outcome metrics.
4. Institutional datasets and technical reports.

## Search Procedure
1. Search strongest academic answer.
2. Search subgroup effects and distributional harms.
3. Search incentive, metric, and legitimacy conflicts.
4. Search whether tradeoffs are alignable or structurally opposed.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 8–10, ceiling 12–15 collision cards.

## Collision Card Gate
Each card must include:
- stakeholder pair or group
- collision mechanism
- linked claim
- source
- verbatim quote
- method basis
- align/compensate/constrain/split recommendation
- decision impact
- confidence
