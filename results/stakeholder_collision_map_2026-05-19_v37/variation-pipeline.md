# Variation Pipeline — Stakeholder Collision Map

Parent: ARCHITECTURE.md

## Status
Created v37 variation centered on mapping stakeholder conflicts, aligned incentives, externalities, and legitimacy risks.

## Pipeline
1. Parse brief into candidate action, affected groups, success metric per group, harm metric per group, power asymmetry, and likely conflict surface.
2. Spawn three isolated lanes.
3. Force each lane to emit collision cards instead of generic findings.
4. Validate stakeholder pair or group, collision mechanism, quote, recommendation, decision impact, and confidence.
5. Audit URLs, quote matches, stakeholder specificity, and Edge redundancy.
6. Build stakeholder collision map:
   - aligned incentive
   - direct conflict
   - hidden externality
   - metric collision
   - power asymmetry
   - trust gap
   - evidence preference clash
   - negotiable tradeoff
7. Synthesize recommendation by stakeholder impact rather than single aggregate benefit.
8. Re-fan one unresolved collision if it controls legitimacy or actionability.

## WHY
Stakeholder Collision Map improves reTruth by replacing average-case recommendations with conflict-aware synthesis. Many actions are good under one metric and harmful under another. This variation forces each lane to say who benefits, who pays, who distrusts the evidence, and whether alignment, compensation, constraint, or split decision is needed.

## Difference From Original
Original architecture separates evidence lanes by source type and cognitive style. Stakeholder Collision Map separates impacts by affected group, then recombines evidence through legitimacy and tradeoff analysis.

## Expected Benefit
- Less one-size-fits-all advice.
- Better detection of hidden externalities.
- Stronger policy, product, governance, and organizational recommendations.
- Clearer conflict resolution options.

## Tradeoff
Can make synthesis more political and less concise. Best for decisions involving multiple affected groups; less useful for purely technical facts with aligned stakeholders.

## Main Risk
Inventing stakeholder conflict where evidence only supports mild preference differences. Mitigation: every collision card must name mechanism, affected group, quote, and align/compensate/constrain/split recommendation.

## Next Variation Ideas
1. Blind Spot Inventory: explicitly catalog absent evidence and unreachable source zones.
2. Robustness Ladder: grade claims by how many attack classes they survive.
3. Incentive Gradient: model how actor behavior changes after recommendation adoption.
