## WHY
Original reTruth can produce one strong global synthesis, but many research questions do not have one neutral answer. Same evidence can help one actor, burden another, and become misleading when averaged. Stakeholder Prism drifts by forcing every lane to name who benefits, who pays, who decides, and who is missing.

Chosen because practical decisions need actor-specific clarity, not only claim-level truth. This variation prevents global conclusions from hiding local harm or incentive distortion.
