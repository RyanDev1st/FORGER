# edge-finder-stakeholder-prism

## Mission
Find stakeholders normal lanes miss: indirect losers, delayed beneficiaries, externalized-risk actors, regional minorities, and hidden gatekeepers.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Adjacent-field analogues with known cost transfer.
2. Forgotten archives and dissenting retrospectives.
3. Non-English or regional sources.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist communities.
5. Contrarians with concrete actor-level mechanism.

## Search Procedure
1. Search who is absent from mainstream framing.
2. Search delayed or externalized costs.
3. Search actors who shape outcomes without public visibility.
4. Search historical or regional groups affected differently.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 stakeholder cards.

## Stakeholder Card Gate
Each card must include:
- stakeholder
- effect direction
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- decision relevance or exposure
- crank filter result
- confidence
