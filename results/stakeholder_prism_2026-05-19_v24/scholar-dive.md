# scholar-dive-stakeholder-prism

## Mission
Find how academic evidence changes when grouped by population, role, cohort, institution, or exposure rather than treated as one average effect.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses with subgroup analysis.
2. Peer-reviewed studies with population splits or heterogeneity.
3. Policy, institutional, and public datasets.
4. Replication, contradiction, and equity-impact studies.

## Search Procedure
1. Search strongest average-effect claims.
2. Search subgroup heterogeneity and exposure splits.
3. Search who benefits, who is harmed, and who is excluded.
4. Search institutional incentives that bias reported outcomes.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 8–10, ceiling 12–15 stakeholder cards.

## Stakeholder Card Gate
Each card must include:
- stakeholder
- effect direction
- linked claim
- source
- verbatim quote
- method basis
- decision relevance or exposure
- asymmetry or tradeoff
- confidence
