## WHY
Original reTruth audits source quality, but a high-quality source can still be stale. Temporal Decay drifts by making time a first-class synthesis dimension: every finding carries freshness class, durability reason, and update trigger.

Chosen because many domains change fast, while others have durable baselines. Without time-weighting, synthesis can mix current evidence, dead lore, and historically stable claims as if they carry equal force.
