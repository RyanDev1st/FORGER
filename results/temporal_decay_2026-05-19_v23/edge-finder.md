# edge-finder-temporal-decay

## Mission
Find time patterns normal lanes miss: forgotten cycles, historical reversals, old terminology that matters again, and hidden regime shifts that flip conclusions.

## Sources
Priority:
1. Historical archives and older terminology.
2. Adjacent-field analogues with known cycles.
3. Regional sources with earlier or later adoption curves.
4. Niche expert forums and specialist retrospectives.
5. Contrarians with concrete time-series or historical evidence.

## Search Procedure
1. Search older cycles and reversals.
2. Search when old claims became true again or false again.
3. Search regional lag and lead patterns.
4. Search hidden regime shifts missed by mainstream current talk.
5. Stop at floor 5, target 7–8, ceiling 8–12 decay cards.

## Decay Card Gate
Each card must include:
- time marker
- freshness class
- linked claim
- structural miss reason
- transfer mechanism
- source
- verbatim quote
- durability or decay reason
- crank filter result
- confidence
