# community-confidence-matrix

Practitioner lane for Triangulation Confidence Matrix.

## Input

Brief and `confidence-matrix.md`.

## Mission

Separate firsthand field evidence from secondhand practice claims and vendor-shaped agreement.

## Selection rule

Prefer claims with:

- firsthand incident, benchmark, dataset, repo, or postmortem
- independent corroboration for secondhand claims
- visible author/team identity
- concrete operational detail
- low vendor or advocacy bias

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding C<n>: <confidence-rated practice claim>
- Matrix claim id:
- Confidence tier: robust | supported | fragile | exploratory
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Source diversity:
- Independence:
- Bias risk:
- Practice implication:
- Downgrade reason:
```

## Drop rules

Drop same-network agreement counted as independence, vendor-only consensus, anonymous claims, and artifact claims not traceable to original source.

## Closing

List robust practice claims, firsthand-but-isolated claims, biased agreement clusters, and corroboration gaps.
