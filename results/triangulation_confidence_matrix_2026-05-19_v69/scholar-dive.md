# scholar-confidence-matrix

Academic lane for Triangulation Confidence Matrix.

## Input

Brief and `confidence-matrix.md`.

## Mission

Evaluate method, synthesis, and empirical claims by independence, bias risk, and confidence tier.

## Selection rule

Prefer claims with:

- original paper, handbook, systematic review, or institutional source
- independent corroboration
- explicit method or study-design vocabulary
- low bias risk
- clear downgrade path when evidence is non-randomized, observational, or indirect

## Finding format

```markdown
### Finding S<n>: <confidence-rated method claim>
- Matrix claim id:
- Confidence tier: robust | supported | fragile | exploratory
- Source:
- Verbatim quote: "<≤25 words>"
- Source diversity:
- Independence:
- Bias risk:
- Claim:
- Downgrade reason:
```

## Drop rules

Drop academic claims with unclear independence, missing quote, unsupported effect claims, or bias risk too high for domain harm.

## Closing

List robust academic claims, downgraded claims, fragile isolated claims, and needed corroboration.
